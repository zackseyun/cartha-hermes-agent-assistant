---
name: experiment-tracker
description: "Track falsifiable growth experiments with baseline, decision rule and evidence, without inventing results."
---

Use record_experiment and get_experiment_results. Require segment, hypothesis, metric/denominator, baseline or explicitly unknown, success rule, stop rule, review date with offset, and evidence source. Proposed is the default; never mark running just because code shipped. Record minimum evidence/cohort maturity in success_rule. Keep user activation, retention, attribution and theological/editorial quality distinct. Do not assert causal uplift from an uncontrolled before/after comparison. Decide continue/change/stop only against declared criteria; missing telemetry means inconclusive. Product direction, clinical claims, asset-sale pricing and resource commitments stay with Zack.
