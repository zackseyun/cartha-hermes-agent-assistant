Call founder_brief. Lead with the three decisions/actions most worth Zack's attention; then Growth, Sales, Experiments, and Blockers. Keep it under 350 words. Name the observation date, source coverage and staleness. Growth defaults to direct PostHog aggregates. State queried_at, the rolling window, provider_last_refresh and served_from_cache; the cache lasts at most five minutes. If connection is report_fallback, disclose the live failure and old report date. The independent noon email stays untouched. Unknown values stay unavailable, not zero. No follow-ups due does not mean the entire real-world pipeline is up to date. Explain that imported research leads are not warm prospects. Do not manufacture sample people, funnel metrics or experiment lifts. Quote errors as observations, not diagnoses. No outbound messages, launches, deployments, investments, transfers or deletion. Propose engineering requests only when grounded in evidence. Do not repeatedly recreate existing requests.
