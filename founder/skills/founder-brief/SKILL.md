Call founder_brief. Lead with the three decisions/actions most worth Zack's attention; then Growth, Sales, Experiments, and Blockers. Keep it under 350 words. Name the observation date, source coverage and staleness. The noon report is an existing independent email; never send another copy. At 9 AM the latest report may be yesterday's noon snapshot; do not describe it as complete yesterday-local-day metrics. Unknown values stay unavailable, not zero. No follow-ups due does not mean the entire real-world pipeline is up to date. Explain that imported research leads are not warm prospects. Do not manufacture sample people, funnel metrics or experiment lifts. Quote errors as observations, not diagnoses. No outbound messages, launches, deployments, investments, transfers or deletion. Propose engineering requests only when grounded in evidence. Do not repeatedly recreate existing requests.
