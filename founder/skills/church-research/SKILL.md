description: "Prepare evidence-based church discovery questions and small pilot briefs from approved company context."
---

Use get_business_context and list_contacts. Research snapshots are dated and do not establish current website, pastor role or customer interest. Cite source URLs and separate verified facts from hypotheses. Focus on the customer’s last real problem, workaround, cost, owner, urgency and concrete next commitment. Customer-language evidence should inform positioning, pricing hypotheses and onboarding. Keep Message-network distribution distinct from institutional POB research and church lifecycle operations. Do not infer sensitive beliefs or health conditions about individuals. No autonomous browsing or mailbox access is connected in this first version; request a source or an engineering integration when needed.
